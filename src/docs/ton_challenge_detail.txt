The TON x Ignyte Global Challenge is designed to accelerate the adoption of Web3 payments as a natural part of everyday life inside Telegram — the world’s most dynamic social platform. This global program invites innovators, startups, and scale-ups to build Web3 products for Business, Payments, and Tokenization.

From splitting bills with friends, to building plug-and-play tools for merchants to reimagine ownership and value by tokenizing assets, the challenge seeks to reimagine how TON can unlock the next wave of mainstream crypto adoption. Winning teams will gain access to TON’s ecosystem, mentorship from industry leaders, and opportunities to pilot their solutions with global communities of users, creators, and enterprises.

Problem Statement
Theme	Problem Statement	Focus Areas
Crypto infrastructure for business	Empower businesses to go fully on-chain. Build plug-and-play solutions that let malls, airlines, cafés, and small shops in Dubai accept crypto, reward customers, and run loyalty programs.	• Design the next-generation on-chain loyalty engine to power malls, airlines, and hospitality partners • Create a plug-and-play mini-app builder for cafés and small shops to accept TON payments in minutes • Build a frictionless crypto tipping platform for service staff, waiters, hairdressers, and beyond.
Payments	Build seamless real-world crypto payment experiences for millions of users worldwide. Deliver experiences so smooth that using crypto for everyday transactions feels natural. Unlock tokenization not only of the assets themselves, but also of the yield they generate.	• Low-cost international remittances • Bill-splitting (split dinner, rent, or taxi fare among friends using crypto in Telegram groups) • Crypto gift (e.g., send Bitcoin as a gift).
Tokenization	Reinvent how the world owns and trades assets. Create powerful tools to bring any asset, digital or real, on-chain and tradeable. Unlock tokenization not only of the assets themselves, but also of the yield they generate.	• Tokenization of yield from real-world assets and cross-chain DeFi • Tokenization of Telegram assets (e.g., emoji, user activity, creators, social capital) • No-code tools for issuing and trading tokenized assets (e.g., real estate or art).

Submission Criteria
Open to students, startups, and scale-ups worldwide.
Solutions must be deployable on the TON Blockchain Mainnet or Testnet.
Deliverable: A 1–2 page summary and 3–5 slide pitch deck describing the selected theme, user problem, and proposed TON-based solution.
Solution Overview (max 2 pages or 5 slides): Problem statement, proposed solution, and market impact.
Depending on your track, include one of the following:
Idea Track: Concept mockups, flow diagrams, or clickable Figma demo showing user interaction inside Telegram.
Prototype Track: A working prototype with a demo video (maximum 3 minutes).
Scale-up Track: Functional product with user testing or traction evidence.
Demo Requirements: Demonstrate at least one working TON feature such as wallet integration, payments, token issuance, or mini-app interaction.
Include a short demo and technical explanation during judging. Provide a link or video demonstrating the solution (YouTube, Loom, or live demo).
Regulatory compliance: Solutions must adhere to applicable regulations if the product is brought to market.
Selection Criteria
Clear and well-defined Unique Selling Proposition (USP).
Technical innovation leveraging the TON + Telegram ecosystem.
User-friendliness and mass-adoption potential.
Sustainability and scalability of the solution.
Ease of integration into the TON ecosystem.
Judging Criteria
Innovation – 20%
Originality and quality of the solution. How unique is the idea? Has this already been tried? Does it bring something new to TON or significantly improve an existing use case?

TON & Telegram Technology Fit – 20%
Does the product leverage TON and the Telegram ecosystem effectively? Is the solution intuitive, user-centric, and aligned with TON’s strengths (speed, UX, distribution, mini-apps, etc.)?

Feasibility & Execution – 20%
How realistic is implementation and launch? Are the steps clear, logical, and achievable with available TON infrastructure and tooling? Can it be executed without heavy dependency on external blockers?

Market Relevance – 20%
Is the solution viable in key markets?

Sustainability & Scalability – 10%
Does the solution have long-term potential? Can it grow to millions of users or global adoption without breaking economically or technically?

Presentation & Storytelling – 10%
Clarity and persuasiveness of the pitch. How well does the demo or prototype showcase the solution? Does it clearly communicate vision, impact, and alignment with TON?
